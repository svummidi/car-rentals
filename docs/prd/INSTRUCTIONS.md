# Car Management PRD Instructions

## Purpose

Use [`prd-car-management.md`](prd-car-management.md) as the draft product requirements document for stakeholder review. It reflects the information available in the originating issue; it is not evidence of stakeholder approval or implementation completion.

## Review before implementation

1. Locate and confirm the authoritative detailed requirements source. The current draft notes that no separate source document was supplied.
2. Have the Product Owner and relevant Fleet, Dispatch, Maintenance, Customer Operations, Security, and Privacy stakeholders review the requirements and open questions.
3. Confirm each business rule, exception, metric definition, and role permission. Do not treat proposed priorities, workflows, future enhancements, or NFR targets as approved decisions.
4. Record decisions in the PRD, preserving the established rules and numerical thresholds. If a decision is unavailable, retain it as an open question rather than inventing a rule.
5. Reconcile requirement IDs, linked stories, acceptance criteria, business rules, and workflows whenever scope changes.
6. Keep implementation artifacts—application code, APIs, database schemas, and UI designs—out of this PRD; maintain them in their appropriate technical/design documents.

## Editing conventions

- Keep all 17 functional modules represented and preserve traceable FR-CM and US-CM identifiers.
- Label new proposals and assumptions clearly; distinguish confirmed requirements from decisions requiring approval.
- Update the document version and last-updated date when revising substantive content.
- Do not claim stakeholder validation until the named approvers have explicitly reviewed and approved the relevant content.
